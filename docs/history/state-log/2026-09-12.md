   chip values at 200px with an ellipsis, on both tabs. Miles's #91 makes that rule
   load-bearing for rescoping. Resolved as D3 below: the property survives, the sentence is
   what is wrong.
4. **§2.6 does not exist.** Section 2 has no subsections at all, and two retirement targets

5. **Two of v1.4's governing sentences are the product agent's reading, not Miles's ruling** —
   search rescoping, and retiring the Requests tiles' filtering. Both owls asked to be
